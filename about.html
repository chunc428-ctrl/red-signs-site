RED SIGNS is a visual and written study of red signage in New York City. The publication investigates how related reds appear across traffic signs, property notices, subway environments, vehicles, building systems, fire-protection infrastructure, construction sites, storefronts, doors, walls, and other everyday instructions.

The images in the publication include both original and externally sourced material.

Pantone 1805 C is used as a visual reference within the publication because Pantone 1805 has a relationship to printed traffic-control red. It should not be understood as the technical specification for every sign included in the project.

Research, writing, image selection, editing, design, and art direction: Carina Chung
